\section{The qualification boundary}
\label{sec:qualification}

Every study manifest, result artifact and evidence graph records engineering qualification as \estatus{NOT\_GRANTED} (PA-22). Several things leave it untouched:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item reproduction, independent agreement and recomputation equivalence;
  \item complete graph coverage;
  \item a human discrepancy decision, which carries \texttt{qualification\_effect: NONE} by construction;
  \item promotion of a study.
\end{itemize}

Three boundaries follow from this.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \textbf{Tolerances.} Verification tolerances express recomputation equivalence, not engineering acceptance.
  \item \textbf{Agreement.} Agreement between a reproduction and a printed value says that the printed value follows from the printed method. It does not say that the method is valid for a design.
  \item \textbf{Human review.} Physics interpretation, scope changes, interpretation of source ambiguities and discrepancy dispositions remain human responsibilities, and the repository contract names them as such.
\end{itemize}

\engiproof{} therefore does not qualify designs and does not replace project-specific engineering verification (PA-23).
