\section{The qualification boundary}
\label{sec:qualification}

Every study manifest, result artifact and evidence graph records engineering qualification as \estatus{NOT\_GRANTED} (PA-22). Reproduction, independent agreement and recomputation equivalence leave it untouched, as do complete graph coverage, a human discrepancy decision (which carries \texttt{qualification\_effect: NONE} by construction) and the promotion of a study.

Three boundaries follow. Verification tolerances express recomputation equivalence, not engineering acceptance. Agreement between a reproduction and a printed value says that the printed value follows from the printed method, not that the method is valid for a design. Physics interpretation, scope changes, the interpretation of source ambiguities and discrepancy dispositions remain human responsibilities, and the repository contract names them as such. \engiproof{} therefore does not qualify designs and does not replace project-specific engineering verification (PA-23).
