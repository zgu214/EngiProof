\section{Source-bounded reproduction}
\label{sec:reproduction}

A \eclass{PUBLISHED} implementation is built only from what the source states: its equations, tables, figure axes and stated load cases. Inputs the source does not give are neither inferred nor tuned (PA-21). Outcomes are recorded per target, never per paper (PA-06, PA-07).

Across the six studies there are 38 comparison records:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item 14 \estatus{REPRODUCED};
  \item 19 \estatus{COMPARED};
  \item 4 \estatus{CONDITIONAL};
  \item 1 \estatus{BLOCKED}.
\end{itemize}

Three behaviours matter for the argument of this paper.

\emph{First}, reproduction is reported target by target. P40, for example, reproduces three of its four equation families to table precision. The fourth disagrees for one of three cases, and that disagreement becomes a discrepancy (\S\ref{sec:discrepancy}); it is not averaged away.

\emph{Second}, a literal implementation that does not close against a figure is not assumed to be a source error. In P42, a literal implementation of the Eqs.~(27)--(30) contact-pressure recursion, followed by Eq.~(41), does not reproduce the Figure~16 moment scale under the stated zero-pressure load case. It is recorded as a \emph{source-implementation provenance gap}: the printed equations do not fix the interface convention. No friction coefficient or contact pressure is adjusted.

\emph{Third}, non-reproduction is an evidence-bearing outcome (PA-20). P45's nonlinear dynamic responses (Figures~4--9 and 11) are \estatus{BLOCKED}, and the record names what is missing:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item the integration parameters $\theta$, $\beta$ and $\lambda$;
  \item the iteration tolerance;
  \item structural damping;
  \item the discretisation of the comparison cases;
  \item the float properties;
  \item the tension unit.
\end{itemize}
The reconstructed integrator also shows why one of these gaps matters: with $\theta=1$ the scheme is unstable above $\Delta t/T = 0.5513$, while $\theta=1.37$ is stable on the tested grid.
