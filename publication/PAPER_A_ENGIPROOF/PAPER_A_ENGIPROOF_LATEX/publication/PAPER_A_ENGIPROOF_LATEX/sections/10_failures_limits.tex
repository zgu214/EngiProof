\subsection{Legitimately irreproducible targets}
Four of the six studies have a major target that cannot be reproduced from the source: the P41 global-buckling FE response, the P42 FE unit-cell results, the P44 nonlinear FE contact profiles and the P45 dynamic responses. These are recorded as limitations or as \estatus{BLOCKED} comparisons, and no inputs are inferred to complete them. Only P45 records its non-reproduced targets as \estatus{BLOCKED} comparisons; the other three record them as limitations, and aligning them is a housekeeping item.

\subsection{Limitations}
No extraction accuracy or recall is measured, and inconsistency detection is not claimed to be complete. The independent checks are methodological, not organisational, and external replication remains future work. The six studies share one domain, and \eclass{SOLVER\_NEW} evidence is defined but not yet exercised. Human decisions have been exercised on eight records by one reviewer, who is also the author; no external reviewer has been involved yet. The source format is recorded for one study only, and most frozen artifacts do not declare the environment that produced them.
