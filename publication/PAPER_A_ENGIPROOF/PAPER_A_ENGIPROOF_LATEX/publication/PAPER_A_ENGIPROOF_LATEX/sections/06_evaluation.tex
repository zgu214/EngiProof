\section{Evaluation strategy}
The final evaluation will be based on completed evidence chains rather than paper count. The current draft uses P40 and P41 as development cases, while additional heterogeneous cases are required before generality claims are made.

Planned evaluation dimensions include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item source identity integrity and fingerprint matching;
  \item selected-target structural readiness;
  \item reproducibility of published numerical relationships;
  \item independent-check agreement;
  \item discrepancy detection and preservation;
  \item provenance/evidence-graph coverage;
  \item backward compatibility of existing studies; and
  \item ability to expose a callable method without overstating qualification.
\end{itemize}

No extraction success percentage is reported in this draft because the denominator and study-selection protocol are not yet fixed. A third mechanically different paper will be used next to stress-test the workflow beyond the two PIP examples.
