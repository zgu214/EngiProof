\section{Evaluation across six studies, by capability}
\label{sec:evaluation}

The evaluation asks which capabilities the six studies exercise, and how strongly; paper, test and file counts are not treated as evidence of validity. The studies were chosen to differ in era, format, mechanics and evidence type (Table~\ref{tab:cases}), but all belong to one domain, offshore and subsea structural mechanics (PA-27).

\subsection{What each case contributes}
Each case illustrates a different capability. In P40, independent evidence decides a conflict: one of the reproduced ratios disagrees with the printed table beyond its rounding band, a different mechanics path (work balance) sides with the reproduction, and a human then recorded a \estatus{DEFERRED} decision that kept the block. In P41, the source contradicts itself: the printed force components do not sum to the printed total, and a paragraph unit contradicts the plotted axis. Both conflicts are preserved, and the plausible 153$\to$159 correction is not applied. P42 shows disagreement that is not an error. Its analytical and FE unit-cell models differ in kinematics, as the source itself explains, and a literal equation chain that fails to close against a figure is recorded as a provenance gap rather than a source error.

P43 tests scale and precision. A full 35-row, 175-eigenvalue table is regenerated by an independent method, and the two printed values that disagree are isolated by three mutually consistent internal checks; this is also the case that required a declared recomputation tolerance with a published-precision guard. P44 contains an ambiguity that nobody can resolve: two incompatible printed time pairs remain open because no EngiProof evidence can arbitrate. The source also calls $K_0$ negligible without giving a value, and none is invented. P45 is a scanned, incomplete source. Automated readiness reached only 4 of 16 targets and the rest was reviewed manually, six discrepancies of five kinds are recorded, and the central dynamic results are \estatus{BLOCKED} with the missing inputs named.

\subsection{What the set does not stress}
The set has clear limits. All six cases come from one domain. No case treats experimental data as its own population: the repository has one model-versus-experiment record (P38-D002) outside the six cases, and the table ratios of P40 are analytical over experimental by construction (PA-26, partially supported). No study produces \eclass{SOLVER\_NEW} evidence (the \eclass{SOLVER\_NEW} class is reserved), extraction accuracy is not measured, and the independence of the checks is methodological rather than organisational. Adding one more paper would close none of these gaps: the first four need corpora, solver adapters or labelled benchmarks, and the last needs external replicators. For that reason no further case was added for this paper.
