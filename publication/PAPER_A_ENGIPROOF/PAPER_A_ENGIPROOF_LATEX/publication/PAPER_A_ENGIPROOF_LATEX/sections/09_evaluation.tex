\section{Evaluation across six studies, by capability}
\label{sec:evaluation}

The evaluation asks which capabilities the six studies exercise, and how strongly. Paper, test and file counts are not treated as evidence of validity. The studies were chosen to differ in era, format, mechanics and evidence type (Table~\ref{tab:cases}). All belong to one domain, offshore and subsea structural mechanics (PA-27).

\subsection{What each case contributes}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.5em, style=nextline]
  \item[P40 --- independent evidence decides a conflict.] Of the reproduced ratios, one disagrees with the printed table beyond its rounding band. A different mechanics path (work balance) sides with the reproduction. A human then recorded a \estatus{DEFERRED} decision, and the block was kept.
  \item[P41 --- a source contradicts itself.] The printed force components do not sum to the printed total, and a paragraph unit contradicts the plotted axis. Both are preserved. The plausible 153$\to$159 correction is not applied.
  \item[P42 --- disagreement that is not an error.] The analytical and FE unit-cell models differ in kinematics, as the source itself explains. A literal equation chain fails to close against a figure, which is recorded as a provenance gap, not as a source error.
  \item[P43 --- scale and precision.] A full 35-row, 175-eigenvalue table is regenerated by an independent method. The two printed values that disagree are isolated by three mutually consistent internal checks. This case is also the one that required a declared recomputation tolerance with a published-precision guard.
  \item[P44 --- an ambiguity nobody can resolve.] Two incompatible printed time pairs remain open, because no EngiProof evidence can arbitrate. The source calls $K_0$ negligible without giving a value, and none is invented.
  \item[P45 --- a scanned, incomplete source.] Automated readiness reached only 4 of 16 targets, and the rest was reviewed manually. Six discrepancies of five kinds are recorded. The central dynamic results are \estatus{BLOCKED} with the missing inputs named.
\end{description}

\subsection{What the set does not stress}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \textbf{Domain.} All six cases come from one domain.
  \item \textbf{Experimental data.} No case treats experimental data as its own population. The repository has one model-versus-experiment record (P38-D002) outside the six cases, and P40's table ratios are analytical over experimental by construction (PA-26, partially supported).
  \item \textbf{\eclass{SOLVER\_NEW}.} No study produces \eclass{SOLVER\_NEW} evidence.
  \item \textbf{Extraction accuracy} is not measured.
  \item \textbf{Independence} of the checks is methodological, not organisational.
\end{itemize}
Adding one more paper would not close any of these gaps. The first four need corpora, solver adapters or labelled benchmarks, and the last needs external replicators. For that reason no further case was added for this paper.
