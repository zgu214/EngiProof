\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\engiproof{} converts selected published engineering methods into source-bounded evidence while keeping separate what was published, what was reproduced, what was checked independently, what disagrees, what a human decided and what remains unqualified. The six heterogeneous studies support four statements. Bounded targets are reproduced to source precision, and the outcome is recorded target by target. Methodologically independent checks decide the direction of numerical conflicts without tuning. Source inconsistencies and missing inputs are preserved as evidence and gate promotion until a human decides. Verification recomputes in isolation and classifies differences across operating systems without mutating the evidence it checks.

The strongest conclusion is methodological: engineering evidence automation should make disagreement and incompleteness explicit and traceable, and a paper-derived method should not be promoted because its code runs or because a printed number can be made to reappear.

Before submission, the tables and the claim--evidence appendix are to be regenerated from a tagged release, and the author statements are to be confirmed.
