\section{Limitations}
The current framework and manuscript have several important limitations:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item PDF mathematical text can be corrupted by embedded fonts and extraction order.
  \item Equation recovery can identify a source block without guaranteeing mathematically correct transcription.
  \item Complex tables, merged headers, and figure axes remain harder than simple equation/table cases.
  \item Symbol-definition extraction remains heuristic.
  \item The framework does not infer genuinely missing source inputs; such cases remain conditional or blocked.
  \item Independent agreement does not prove that a published interpretation is correct.
  \item Reproduction and computational verification do not constitute engineering qualification.
  \item The present automation evidence is still concentrated in structural/subsea engineering examples.
  \item The P40 and P41 mismatch interpretations remain open unless supported by additional provenance such as errata or author clarification.
  \item No global or local FE model is recreated for P41 Figures 8--10 in the present checkpoint.
\end{enumerate}
