\section{Introduction}
Engineering papers often contain enough information to reproduce part of a method, but not necessarily enough information to reproduce a complete model, resolve every convention, or qualify a result for design use. Reuse therefore requires more than extracting equations or generating executable code. A defensible workflow must preserve the identity of the source, distinguish published reference data from independent calculations, expose assumptions and unavailable inputs, quantify comparisons, and retain unresolved discrepancies.

This distinction becomes important when a paper is internally inconsistent. A conventional paper-to-code workflow may silently select one source value, reinterpret a unit, or tune an input until the generated code reproduces the printed result. Such behavior can produce an apparently successful program while weakening engineering traceability. \engiproof{} instead treats disagreement as evidence that must be classified and preserved.

The framework is motivated by the principle
\begin{center}
\textbf{published research $\rightarrow$ engineering evidence $\rightarrow$ callable engineering method,}
\end{center}
not by paper-to-code conversion alone. The intended contribution is therefore an evidence progression model: what was published, what was independently reconstructed, what was newly generated, what was compared, what remains unresolved, and what has not been engineering-qualified.

The present manuscript focuses on the architecture and on two real-source development cases. The first is propagation buckling in subsea pipe-in-pipe (PIP) systems \cite{Karampour2017}. The second is global buckling and structural response of PIP systems \cite{Goplen2011}. The cases are intentionally used not only to demonstrate agreement, but also to test whether the framework can detect and preserve source-backed discrepancies.

The current bounded contributions are:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em]
  \item a source-bounded ingestion and provenance workflow;
  \item explicit separation of extraction candidates, evidence classes, evidence status, and engineering qualification;
  \item failure-aware equation and table recovery with selected-target readiness gates;
  \item comparison and discrepancy records that preserve disagreement rather than tune it away;
  \item append-only human discrepancy decisions and evidence-graph synchronization; and
  \item development-case evidence showing both successful reproduction and source-backed mismatch detection.
\end{enumerate}
