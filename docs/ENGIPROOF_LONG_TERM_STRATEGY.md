# EngiProof Long-Term Strategy
## From Personal Engineering Project to Engineering-Evidence Methodology

**Status:** Strategic direction  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Owner:** Zhiqiang Gu

---

## 1. Core strategic principle

> **The journal paper is one layer of the moat, not the moat itself. The deeper moat is the accumulated, verified engineering evidence system—and the ability to keep extending it faster and more rigorously than others can reproduce it.**

> **期刊论文只是护城河的一层，而不是护城河本身。更深的护城河，是持续积累、经过验证的工程证据体系，以及我们比别人更快、更严谨地扩展这个体系的能力。**

EngiProof should not define itself as a “paper-to-code” tool. Its long-term identity is:

> **EngiProof is an engineering evidence system.**

The product converts published engineering research into source-linked, runnable, reproducible, independently checked, discrepancy-aware computational evidence with explicit qualification boundaries.

---

## 2. The moat is cumulative, not singular

The long-term moat is layered:

1. **Journal papers and scholarly recognition** — establish priority, terminology, peer review and a citable methodology.
2. **Public software and interfaces** — demonstrate that the methodology is executable, not conceptual.
3. **Verified engineering evidence corpus** — each study preserves source identity, runnable calculations, independent checks, comparisons, discrepancies and evidence status.
4. **Engineering judgment rules** — Candidate extraction ≠ Evidence; Code execution ≠ Verification; Reproduction ≠ Independent validation; Verification ≠ Engineering qualification; never tune missing inputs to force agreement; preserve PUBLISHED / INDEPENDENT / SOLVER_NEW.
5. **Engineering know-how** — knowing what to reproduce, what not to infer, what differences matter, and when evidence is insufficient.
6. **Accumulated evidence history** — source fingerprints, test histories, discrepancy decisions, qualification boundaries and cross-paper relationships.
7. **Workflow and ecosystem** — CLI, API, GUI, reporting, review workflows and solver integration.
8. **Sustained development velocity** — competitors may reproduce an older generation while EngiProof keeps moving forward.

## 3. Reality of a one-person project

EngiProof is currently a personal project. That is not automatically a weakness.

A single engineer can realistically build the **0→1 foundation** and a substantial part of the **1→10 system** if the work is highly automated, tightly scoped and evidence-driven.

A single person can realistically create:
- the architecture;
- the evidence model;
- provenance rules;
- discrepancy taxonomy;
- the first journal methodology paper;
- a strong benchmark library;
- a working software product;
- tens of deeply reviewed studies;
- potentially hundreds of partially automated evidence cases over time;
- the vocabulary and design rules of a new engineering-evidence category.

However, some outcomes cannot be created by one person alone:
- a genuine engineering community;
- independent external replication;
- broad industrial trust;
- formal recommended practice;
- standards-body adoption;
- large-scale qualification across many organizations.

Those require other people by definition.

Therefore the strategic objective is **not to do everything alone forever**.

> **Build far enough, rigorously enough and visibly enough that joining, using, citing or extending EngiProof becomes rational for other engineers and researchers.**

## 4. Solo scaling model

### 0 → 1: Can be done personally
Build the core software, evidence lifecycle, provenance controls, continuity architecture, first heterogeneous benchmark studies and first methodology paper.

### 1 → 10: Still primarily personal
Build robust ingestion, reusable engineering kernels, discrepancy automation, evidence graph, 20–50 high-quality heterogeneous studies and the first serious external release.

### 10 → 100: Requires automation more than headcount
Scale through automatic source discovery, target localization, reusable templates, automated comparison generation, checkpoint automation and selective human review only at genuine engineering-judgment gates.

### 100 → 1000: Requires ecosystem
External contributors, users, reviewers and organizations become increasingly important. The founder should no longer personally review every item; the methodology and tooling should make distributed contribution possible without losing evidence quality.

### Standardization stage
Formal influence on engineering practice requires independent users, independent replication, documented failure cases, multiple organizations, public benchmark evidence and sustained credibility.

## 5. A realistic long-term target

The statement:

> “I have five years of verified engineering evidence, hundreds of benchmark cases, thousands of reviewed discrepancies, a trusted workflow, an engineering community, and the next generation is already under development.”

should be interpreted as a **long-term system outcome**, not as a requirement that one person manually performs every action.

A realistic decomposition is:
- **one person creates the architecture and early evidence base;**
- **automation multiplies the person’s effective capacity;**
- **the journal methodology creates academic legitimacy;**
- **the software creates usability;**
- **the evidence corpus creates cumulative technical value;**
- **external users create the community;**
- **continued development preserves the lead.**

The personal project is the seed, not necessarily the final organizational form.

## 7. Publication strategy

The first journal paper should establish and test the methodology, not claim the entire long-term vision is complete.

A suitable central thesis is:

> **EngiProof introduces a controlled engineering-evidence lifecycle in which source identity, published methods, independent computations, discrepancies, human decisions, callable implementations and qualification status remain explicitly linked and machine-auditable.**

The journal paper provides scholarly recognition, methodological priority, peer review, external scrutiny and a citable basis for future adoption.

It is a layer of the moat, not the moat itself.

## 9. Founder rule

Do not measure progress by whether other people already believe in the project.

Measure progress by accumulated evidence:
- Is the method reproducible?
- Does it survive heterogeneous papers?
- Does it detect real discrepancies?
- Are the boundaries honest?
- Is the software becoming easier to use?
- Is the evidence corpus growing?
- Can another competent person resume and verify the work?
- Are external users beginning to reproduce or cite the methodology?

Belief should follow evidence, not replace it.

---

## 10. Long-term founder principle

> **A personal project does not need to remain a one-person project forever. The founder’s job is to build enough verified value that the project eventually becomes larger than the founder.**

> **个人项目不意味着永远只能靠一个人。创始人的任务，是先建立足够多、足够可信、足够难以复制的验证价值，让项目最终能够成长为超越个人的体系。**

EngiProof should continue to optimize for rigor, traceability, automation, evidence accumulation, reproducibility, honest qualification boundaries and development velocity.
