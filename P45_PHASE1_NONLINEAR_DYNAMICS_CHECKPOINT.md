## Reproduced / independently checked

- Table 2: riser length = depth + 15.24 − 9.144 m and static offset = 3% of depth reproduce all three depths to print rounding.
- Tables 1–2 and figure axes are SI renderings of round imperial quantities (max deviation 0.48%).
- Appendix 1: k3 = k1 + k2 in all branches; the compression/tension branches converge to the zero-force branch at O((αL)²) including shear; k1 = k2 at the pinned–pinned Euler load; an independent Hermite beam-column FE solution agrees to < 1e-7; the 12×12 K has exactly six rigid-body modes (four if k3 is perturbed 1%).
- Eq. (7) reduces exactly to the Wilson-θ linear-acceleration relations. A reconstructed SDOF scheme reproduces the textbook stability limits (θ = 1: Δt/T = 0.5513; θ ≥ 1.37 stable). The source does not give θ.
- Appendix 2: C-factors are linear/Hermite interpolation at segment midpoints; B_i is the transverse projector ρSΔl·Cm(I − e eᵀ).
- Infinite-depth Airy model valid at all depths (1 − tanh kh ≤ 5.3e-7).
